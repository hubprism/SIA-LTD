Who We Are

An independent advisory firm, for the whole brief.

NGOs — from Nigeria to clients around the world, helping them turn sustainability, ESG, environmental health and development commitments into programmes that are funded, credible and delivered.

We are not a subsidiary, a spin-off, or a satellite office of a larger firm elsewhere. That independence is deliberate. It means every recommendation we make is built around your outcomes, not a parent agenda, a global template, or a house methodology that ignores the realities on the ground, wherever that ground may be.

Our Approach

Most firms specialise. We integrate.

Climate risk, ESG strategy, environmental health, and monitoring & evaluation are typically treated as separate capabilities, managed by different vendors, on different timelines, with separate accountability. That's where good programmes lose momentum.

SIA is built to close that gap. One team, one point of accountability, across:

— Risk assessment, adaptation planning, climate finance readiness

— Strategy, reporting, and roadmaps built to withstand scrutiny

— Impact assessments and compliance advisory

— MEL systems and evidence generation

— Capacity-building that outlasts the engagement

— Delivery support from design to close-out
HOW WE WORK

Independent. Rigorous. Global.

Independent

We are unaffiliated by design. No parent company's agenda sits between our advice and your outcomes — our only mandate is doing right by the brief.

Rigorous

Our recommendations are built on evidence, not templates. Every engagement — from a single environmental health assessment to a multi-year, donor-funded programme — gets the same standard of technical rigour.

Global

We are based in Nigeria and structured to deliver wherever the brief takes us. That grounding means we understand regulatory environments and funding landscapes from the ground up — and we bring the same rigour to clients anywhere.
