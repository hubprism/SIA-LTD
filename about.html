About SIA

We exist because commitments without delivery are just paperwork.

Sustainable Impact Advisory was built on a simple frustration: too many climate, ESG and development commitments stall between the strategy document and the ground. We close that gap.

See what we do Start a conversation
Who We Are

An independent advisory firm, for the whole brief.

NGOs — from Nigeria to clients around the world — helping them turn sustainability, ESG, environmental health and development commitments into programmes that are funded, credible and delivered.

We are not a subsidiary, a spin-off, or a satellite office of a larger firm elsewhere. That independence is deliberate. It means every recommendation we make is built around your outcomes — not a parent agenda, a global template, or a house methodology that ignores the realities on the ground, wherever that ground may be.

Our Approach

Most firms specialise. We integrate.

Climate risk, ESG strategy, environmental health, and monitoring & evaluation are typically treated as separate capabilities — managed by different vendors, on different timelines, with separate accountability. That's where good programmes lose momentum.
