About SIA

We exist because commitments without delivery are just paperwork.

Sustainable Impact Advisory was built on a simple frustration: too many climate, ESG and development commitments stall between the strategy document and the ground. We close that gap.

Explore our services Chat on WhatsApp
Our Approach

One team, so nothing falls through the gaps.

Climate, ESG, environmental health and MEL are usually handled by separate vendors — each with their own scope, timeline and invoice. We built SIA to close that gap: one independent partner that carries a brief from assessment through to funded, delivered programme.

That means fewer handoffs, a consistent evidence base across disciplines, and recommendations that are shaped around your outcomes rather than a parent agency's agenda.

Why Work With Us

Three principles behind every engagement.

Independent

Unaffiliated, by design.

We are not tied to a parent agency, contractor network or donor programme. Our recommendations serve your outcomes — not a wider agenda.

Integrated

One brief, one team.

Climate, ESG, environmental health and MEL expertise sit under one roof, so your programme is planned and delivered with a consistent evidence base.

Regional Reach

Nigeria-based, West Africa-ready.

Headquartered in Abuja and built to scale delivery across West Africa as programmes grow beyond a single country.
